## 1. Introduction & SPMS Cycle Overview

The **BSU Strategic Performance Management System (BSU-SPMS)** is an end-to-end institutional platform implementing the Civil Service Commission (CSC) performance management framework for Benguet State University.

Every evaluation period follows a synchronized, 4-tier institutional cascade across two core phases:

```
[ Phase 1: Target Setting ]
University Goals (OPCR) ➔ College Deans (DPCR) ➔ Department Chairs (DPCR) ➔ Faculty / Staff (IPCR / IPERF)
                                        │
                                        ▼ (Review & Lock)
                              [ Phase 2: Evaluation ]
Accomplishments Reported ➔ MOVs / Drive Proof Attached ➔ Q-E-T Scoring ➔ TWG/PMT Audit ➔ Official CSC Excel
```

### Core Forms Summary
* **OPCR (Office Performance Commitment and Review):** Formulated by Apex Leadership (VPAA / University President) representing institutional commitments.
* **DPCR (Division Performance Commitment and Review):** Formulated by College Deans (Tier 2) and Department Chairs (Tier 3) linking collegiate deliverables to institutional PPAs.
* **IPCR (Individual Performance Commitment and Review):** Formulated by permanent, temporary, and contractual faculty, divided into Core, Strategic, and Support Functions.
* **IPERF (Individual Performance Evaluation & Review Form):** Formulated by Job Order (JO) and Contract of Service (COS) administrative personnel, structured around deliverables and office PPAs.

## 2. Part 1: Faculty & Staff Operations Guide (IPCR & IPERF)

### 1.1 Logging In & Accessing Your Documents
1. Open the BSU-SPMS portal in your web browser.
2. Enter your institutional email address and password.
3. Complete the Cloudflare security turnstile check.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. Upon landing on your Dashboard, look at your **My Performance Documents** table or open the left navigation menu to view your active semester folder.

### 1.2 Formulating Targets & Balancing Weights
1. Open your active evaluation document (IPCR or IPERF).
2. **For IPCR (Faculty):**
   - **Core Functions (Teaching/Instruction):** Input subjects taught, student counts, instructional materials prepared.
   - **Strategic Priorities (Research/Extension):** Input research proposals, published papers, community outreach.
   - **Support Functions:** Input administrative committee work, advisory tasks, or accreditation duties.
3. **For IPERF (Job Order & COS Personnel):**
   - Click **+ Add Deliverable** to add rows. Type in your contractual tasks, PPAs, and measurable outputs.
4. **Balancing Percentage Weights:**
   - In each category header, type in the percentage weight assigned to that category (e.g. Core = 70%, Strategic = 15%, Support = 15%).
   - **Crucial Rule:** The total percentage must equal exactly **100%**. If weights do not add up to 100%, the submission button will indicate a balance error.
5. **Auto-Save Protection:** The system automatically saves your table entries in real-time as you type.

### 1.3 Submitting Targets for Supervisor Review
1. Once your deliverables, success indicators, and percentage weights are finalized, scroll to the top-right header of your matrix.
2. Click the green **Submit Targets** button.
3. A confirmation dialog will appear. Click **Confirm Submission**.
4. **Target Lock:** Once submitted, target editing is locked while your Department Chair, Dean, and assigned TWG reviewer inspect the commitments.
5. **Withdrawing Submission (Unsubmit):** If you accidentally submitted your targets before you were completely finished, you can click the **Unsubmit** button in the top navigation bar at any time before your supervisor approves. This immediately returns your targets to draft mode.
6. **If Changes Are Needed:** Your supervisor or TWG will return the document with inline remarks. You will receive an instant in-app notification. Open your document, review the remarks, edit your targets, and click **Submit Targets** again.

### 1.4 Attaching Verification Proof (MOVs & Google Drive)
The Civil Service Commission requires verifiable **Means of Verification (MOV)** before any accomplishment can be graded.

1. Locate the deliverable row in your matrix.
2. In the **MOV** column, click the **Paperclip icon** (📎).
3. The **MOV Evidence Drawer** will slide open on the right.
4. **Option A — Upload a Local File:**
   - Click the file upload box or drag-and-drop a file.
   - Supported formats: PDF (`.pdf`), Word (`.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`), PNG, JPG.
5. **Option B — Paste a Cloud Storage Link (Recommended for Large Portfolios & Videos):**
   - Upload your portfolio, syllabi folder, or video recording to your **BSU Google Drive**, **Microsoft OneDrive**, or **Dropbox**.
   - Ensure the link sharing permissions are set to *"Anyone with the link can view"* (or restricted to BSU institutional domain).
   - Paste the URL into the **Cloud Storage Link** field and click **Add Link**.
   - The system automatically displays a branded cloud badge (Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox) and renders an inline preview.
6. You can attach multiple files and cloud links to each deliverable row.

### 1.5 Reporting Accomplishments & Self-Rating
At the end of the semester or cycle:
1. Open your approved document.
2. In the **Actual Accomplishments** column, type in what you completed during the semester (e.g., *"Successfully taught 4 subjects with 135 passing students; submitted grade sheets on Dec 14"*).
3. Ensure your MOVs are attached in the adjacent column.
4. In the **Self-Rating** columns, enter your ratings on the **1 to 5 scale**:
   - **Q (Quality):** Accuracy, competence, adherence to standards.
   - **E (Efficiency):** Resource utilization, volume handled.
   - **T (Timeliness):** Completed on or ahead of deadlines.
5. **Dimension Gating (N/A):** If Efficiency or Timeliness is not measurable or applicable for a specific duty, select **N/A**. The formula automatically excludes that dimension and computes the average cleanly.
6. When complete, click **Submit Evaluation** to route your accomplishments to your supervisor. If needed before supervisor review, you can click **Unsubmit** to withdraw back to draft.

### 1.6 Exporting to Official CSC Excel & Printing
1. After your supervisor and evaluator have finalized and approved your ratings, click **Export Excel** in the document viewer header.
2. The system generates an official Civil Service Commission spreadsheet (`.xlsx`) matching the statutory CSC template.
3. Open the downloaded file in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
4. Press <kbd>Ctrl + P</kbd> to print onto standard Legal/A4 paper. All columns, formulas, and margins are pre-formatted with zero cropped text.

## 3. Part 2: Supervisor & Evaluator Operations Guide (Chairs & Deans)

### 2.1 Cascading Institutional Targets Downwards
The SPMS requires strict top-down alignment:
1. **Dean Cascading:** Before a College Dean can cascade to Department Chairs, the Dean's collegiate DPCR must first be submitted, reviewed by supervising authorities, and verified by the TWG.
2. **Department Chair Cascading:** Before a Chair can distribute semester folders to faculty, the Chair's departmental DPCR must first achieve `TARGET APPROVED`.
3. **Distributing to Team Members:**
   - Open your approved DPCR document.
   - Click the **Team / Distribution** button in the header toolbar to open the team drawer.
   - Select the subordinate personnel in your department/college.
   - Click **Cascade / Distribute**. The system clones the approved departmental commitments as the foundational baseline for subordinates.

### 2.2 Reviewing & Approving Subordinate Targets
1. Open your Dashboard or navigate to **Review Queue**.
2. Click on any document marked **Submitted (Target Review)**.
3. Verify that:
   - Deliverables support departmental PPAs.
   - Target metrics are realistic and quantifiable.
   - Percentage category weights equal **100%**.
4. **Sequential Target Approval Pipeline:**
   - **Step A — Supervising Authorities Review First:** The Immediate Supervisor (and next-in-rank superiors if routed) review the document and click **Approve**. Throughout this review period, the master folder status cleanly remains **TARGET SUBMITTED**, with individual approval progress tracked directly on each supervisor's card in Layer 2.
   - **Step B — Forwarding to TWG:** When all routed supervising authorities have approved, an automated notification is sent to the assigned Technical Working Group (TWG) reviewer, unlocking their verification controls (`Approve` and `Return` buttons become active).
   - **Step C — TWG Final Verification:** The assigned TWG audits compliance against university standards and clicks **Approve**. TWG is the **final approver** that officially unlocks the document into **TARGET APPROVED** (`target_approver_role = 'TWG'`), enabling downstream cascading.
5. **Revisions:** If adjustments are needed at any stage, click **Return**. Enter clear, actionable notes explaining what needs modification.
6. **Unapproving:** If an error is noticed after approval, reviewers can click **Unapprove** to pull the document back to pending review (available as long as the deadline window remains active).

### 2.3 Inspecting Evidence & Verification Proof
1. During the evaluation phase, open the subordinate's submitted paper.
2. In the MOV column, click the **Paperclip / View Proof** badge on any deliverable row.
3. Click to preview uploaded PDFs, images, or documents, or click cloud links to view Google Drive folders and portfolios directly.
4. If proof is missing or insufficient, leave an inline review remark requesting the required MOV.

### 2.4 Performance Scoring & Dimension Gating (N/A)
Score each deliverable row across the standard 3 dimensions:
* **Quality (Q):** Excellence, accuracy, zero errors, stakeholder satisfaction.
* **Efficiency (E):** Output volume, optimal resource utilization, standard operating procedures.
* **Timeliness (T):** Meeting or beating statutory and university deadlines.

#### Standard CSC Numerical Rating Scale:
| Numerical Rating | Adjectival Rating | Meaning & Criteria |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **5** | **Outstanding** | Performance exceeded targets by at least 30% with extraordinary quality and zero defects. |
| **4** | **Very Satisfactory** | 100% of agreed commitments were delivered on time with high competence. |
| **3** | **Satisfactory** | Standard acceptable output; met baseline requirements. |
| **2** | **Unsatisfactory** | Substantial portion of commitments were neglected or incomplete. |
| **1** | **Poor** | Severe failures across multiple key duties. |

### 2.5 Calibrating Ratings, Score Adjustments & Audit Dots
When a supervisor or Technical Working Group / Performance Management Team (TWG/PMT) reviewer modifies a ratee's self-rating:
1. Enter the calibrated score directly into the evaluator rating cell.
2. The system flags the cell with an elegant **corner notification dot**:
   - 🟢 **Emerald Dot:** Score adjusted by the Head of Office (Immediate Supervisor).
   - 🟡 **Amber Dot:** Score calibrated by the Technical Working Group / Performance Management Team (TWG/PMT).
3. **Hover Tooltip:** Hovering over the corner dot displays the exact revision trail (`was 4 ➔ now 3`).
4. **3-Tier Rating Attribution Audit Modal:** Clicking the corner dot opens the comprehensive audit popup showing timestamps, previous scores, calibrator identity, and justification notes.

### 2.6 Dynamic Approval Safeguards & Returning for Revision
To protect transparency and due process under CSC guidelines:
* **Dynamic Lock:** If an evaluator modifies any score away from the ratee's submitted baseline, the direct **Approve Rating** button is automatically disabled.
* **Why?** An evaluator cannot unilaterally finalize a disputed score without returning the document to the subordinate for acknowledgment or due process discussion.
* **Resolution Pathways:**
  1. Click **Return for Revision** to send the calibrated score and supervisor remarks back to the subordinate.
  2. Or revert the adjusted score back to the submitted baseline, which instantly re-enables the direct **Approve Rating** button in real time.

## 4. Part 3: HRDO & System Administrator Operations Guide

### 4.1 Setting Up Rating Periods & Semester Folders
1. Navigate to **Folders / Evaluation Cycles** in the administration panel.
2. Click **Create New Period**.
3. Fill in the cycle details:
   - **Academic Year & Semester:** e.g., *1st Semester 2026-2027*.
   - **Target Submission Deadline:** Cutoff date for Phase 1 commitments.
   - **Evaluation & Grading Deadline:** Cutoff date for Phase 2 scoring.
4. Click **Create Cycle**.
5. Once a cycle concludes, use **Archive Period** to seal the records and preserve read-only compliance access.

### 4.2 Managing Organizational Units & Colleges
1. Navigate to **Accounts & Organization ➔ Organizational Units**.
2. **Add Unit / College:** Click **+ Add Unit**. Specify unit name (e.g., *College of Agriculture*), parent unit (*OVPAA* or *Office of the President*), and unit type (*College*, *Department*, or *Administrative Division*).
3. **Update / Reassign Unit:** Change reporting lines or department heads if reorganizations occur.
4. **Delete Unit:** Units with active submitted documents are protected against accidental deletion.

### 4.3 Managing Plantilla Positions
1. Navigate to **Accounts & Organization ➔ Plantilla Catalog**.
2. Click **+ Add Position**.
3. Enter the official government designation (e.g., *Associate Professor V*, *Administrative Aide VI*, *Instructor I*).
4. Specify employment category (*Faculty*, *Staff*, or *Contractual/COS*).
5. These titles automatically populate the official CSC signatory blocks and export headers.

### 4.4 Inviting, Role Assignment & Account Security
1. Navigate to **Accounts ➔ User Directory**.
2. **Send Invites:** Click **Invite Users**. Enter the employee's BSU email, select their designated unit, position, and role.
3. **User Roles:**
   - **User:** Standard faculty or administrative personnel (IPCR/IPERF).
   - **Supervisor:** Department Chairs and College Deans with cascading and rating authority (DPCR).
   - **TWG Reviewer:** Performance Management Team / Technical Working Group calibration audit access.
   - **Admin:** Complete HRDO institutional configuration and override privileges.
4. **Account Toggle:** Click **Toggle Status** to immediately disable or re-enable employee portal access upon separation or leave of absence.

### 4.5 Technical Working Group / Performance Management Team (TWG/PMT) Calibration Assignments
1. In the admin directory, select personnel designated as TWG or PMT reviewers.
2. Assign colleges or units under their monitoring remit.
3. TWG/PMT members gain inspection rights to Stage 2 Target Calibration dashboards and the **+ [TWG]** or **+ [PMT]** official feedback stamp.

## 5. Part 4: Official CSC Excel Exporter & Printing Rules

To ensure 100% compliance with Civil Service Commission documentation audits:
1. **Direct Web Browser Printing is Deprecated:**
   - Browser printing engines (`Ctrl + P` in Chrome/Edge) introduce unpredictable page breaks, cut off deliverable matrices, and crop signature blocks.
2. **Standardized CSC Export:**
   - Always click the green **Export Excel** button in the document toolbar.
   - The system utilizes the official `CscExcelExporter` engine to generate statutory `.xlsx` files with standard BSU green typography, verified category percentage math, and formula protection.
3. **Workbook & Cell Protection:**
   - Downloaded spreadsheets are protected against accidental cell edits and formula tampering.
   - **Default Unlock Password:** `spms`
   - Protection allows full viewing, copying, and clean printing while ensuring archival integrity.
4. **Printing Guidelines from Excel:**
   - Open the `.xlsx` file in Microsoft Excel.
   - Set paper size to **Legal (8.5" x 14")** or **A4**.
   - Page margins and print orientation (*Landscape*) are pre-configured. Simply press <kbd>Ctrl + P</kbd> and click Print.

## 6. Part 5: Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Why is my "Approve Rating" button disabled?
> **Answer:** You have adjusted or calibrated one or more scores away from the baseline submitted by the ratee. The system prevents direct one-click approval to protect due process. You can either:
> 1. Click **Return for Revision** to send the calibrated score and feedback remarks back to the subordinate.
> 2. Or reset your adjustments back to the original submitted rating to re-enable direct approval.

### Q2: Why is my document locked and read-only?
> **Answer:** Once you click **Submit Targets** or **Submit Evaluation**, the matrix is locked to prevent unauthorized tampering during superior review. If you need to make corrections, click **Unsubmit** (if not yet approved) or ask your Department Chair/Dean to click **Return**, which re-opens your document for editing.

### Q3: Why does the system say my weights must equal 100%?
> **Answer:** Under CSC guidelines, the combined percentage weights of your categories (Core Functions + Strategic Priorities + Support Functions) must sum to exactly **100%**. Check the percentage input fields in each category header and ensure they add up to 100.

### Q4: My file is too large to upload in the MOV drawer. What should I do?
> **Answer:** For large portfolios, syllabi binders, or video files, do not upload raw heavy files directly. Upload your file or folder to your **BSU Google Drive**, set the link to *"Anyone with the link can view"*, and paste the URL into the **Cloud Storage Link** field in the MOV drawer.

### Q5: What is the password to unprotect the downloaded Excel sheet?
> **Answer:** The default institutional unlock password for all exported CSC Excel spreadsheets is `spms`.

### Q6: Can I take back a document after accidentally clicking Submit?
> **Answer:** Yes! In both the Target Setting and Evaluation phases, a clean **Unsubmit** button appears in your document header. As long as your supervisor has not yet approved your submission, clicking **Unsubmit** instantly withdraws your document back to editable draft status without requiring supervisor intervention.

### Q7: Where did the browser Print / PDF button go?
> **Answer:** It was intentionally removed and replaced by **Export Excel**. Browser PDF prints frequently broke table layouts and cropped text. The exported Excel file provides 100% CSC fidelity and prints cleanly via <kbd>Ctrl + P</kbd>.

### Q8: What does the "N/A" option mean in the scoring columns?
> **Answer:** "N/A" stands for Dimension Gating. If a specific task cannot be fairly judged on Efficiency (e.g., qualitative student mentorship) or Timeliness (e.g., ongoing research), select **N/A**. The system automatically excludes that dimension from the row average calculation.

### Q9: What happens if the submission deadline passes while my document is still waiting for supervisor or TWG review?
> **Answer:** You do NOT receive a missed deadline penalty! The SPMS deadline safeguard strictly protects on-time submissions. As long as you clicked **Submit** before the deadline cutoff, your document remains safely under active review. The "Missed Deadline" status only applies to ratees who failed to submit their draft before the window closed.
